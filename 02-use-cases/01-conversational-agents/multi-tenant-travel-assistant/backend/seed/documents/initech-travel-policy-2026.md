# Initech — Business Travel Policy 2026

Document version 2026.1 · Owner: Finance Operations · Effective 1 January 2026

*Fictional document for this sample. Not a real corporate policy.*

---

## 1. Principles

Initech travels to meet customers and partners where remote work would not
achieve the same outcome. Every trip should be justifiable on that basis alone.

This policy is deliberately restrictive. Finance Operations would rather answer a
question before a booking than an exception afterwards.

## 2. Air travel

### 2.1 Cabin

**Economy class on all flights, regardless of duration.** There is no cabin
entitlement scheme, no accrual by trip count, and no distinction between
short-haul and long-haul.

The only exception is a documented medical requirement, approved in advance by
Finance Operations with supporting occupational health documentation. Seniority
is not a basis for exception at any grade.

### 2.2 Advance purchase

**Flights must be booked at least seven days before departure.** Bookings inside
seven days require approval from a Director before ticketing.

The seven-day window exists to control fare inflation rather than to discourage
travel. Where a genuine business need arises inside the window — a customer
escalation, a contract signing brought forward — approval is normally granted
promptly. Travellers should not delay a necessary trip to satisfy the window.

### 2.3 Fares

Book the lowest available fare. **Non-refundable fares are the default**, and
refundable fares require justification recorded at the time of booking.

Where a trip is likely to move, the correct approach is to book non-refundable
and re-book if needed, rather than paying a refundable premium in advance. The
cost of an occasional change fee is lower than a routine refundable uplift.

### 2.4 Ancillary expenses

Seat selection fees are not reimbursable. Checked baggage is reimbursable for
trips of four nights or longer. In-flight wifi is reimbursable where required for
scheduled work in transit; note the business reason in the claim.

## 3. Accommodation

### 3.1 Rate caps

The nightly cap is **EUR 150** excluding taxes, and properties above three stars
are outside policy.

The cap is uniform across all cities. Finance Operations does not maintain
city-specific exceptions, because doing so has historically produced more
disputes than it resolved. Where the cap cannot be met, see 3.2.

### 3.2 When the cap cannot be met

If no compliant property is available — a trade fair, a conference, a small city
with limited inventory — book the cheapest available option and record the
circumstance. Attach evidence of the rates you were shown.

Do not book a compliant property an unreasonable distance from the meeting
location in order to satisfy the cap. Two hours of daily travel costs more in
working time than the rate difference saves.

### 3.3 Extras

**Room upgrades are not reimbursable** under any circumstance, including
complimentary upgrades that carry a resort or amenity fee.

Breakfast is reimbursable up to EUR 15 per day where not included. Laundry is
reimbursable on trips of seven nights or longer.

## 4. Ground transport

Public transport is the default for airport transfers where a direct service
exists. Ride-hailing is appropriate outside service hours, with luggage, or where
personal safety is a consideration — the last of these needs no justification.

Rental cars require approval in advance.

## 5. Approvals

| Situation | Approver |
|---|---|
| Inside policy | none required |
| Booking within 7 days of departure | Director |
| Refundable fare | recorded justification |
| Above accommodation cap | Finance Operations, in advance where possible |
| Rental car | line manager |
| Medical cabin exception | Finance Operations + occupational health |

## 6. Expenses and evidence

Submit claims within 30 days of trip completion. Receipts are required for every
item above EUR 25. Missing receipts require a written explanation and are
approved at Finance Operations' discretion.

Claims submitted more than 60 days after travel are not reimbursed except in
documented exceptional circumstances.

## 7. Duty of care

All travel must be booked through the corporate tool so that the company holds an
accurate record of traveller location. Directly booked travel is not covered by
corporate travel insurance.

# Statement of Work — Cloud Modernisation Programme

## 1. Introduction
ContosoTech Consulting will support NorthBank in its journey to the cloud. This
engagement will help the customer modernise their loan origination platform and
achieve greater agility.

## 2. Scope
ContosoTech will provide cloud architecture services, assist with the migration,
and support the customer team throughout. Our consultants will work closely with
NorthBank stakeholders to deliver a modern, scalable solution on AWS.

Activities include:
- Discovery and assessment
- Architecture design
- Migration support
- Knowledge transfer

## 3. Timeline
The engagement is expected to run for approximately 12 weeks.

## 4. Commercials
Services will be provided on a time and materials basis at the agreed rate card.

## 5. General
Both parties will act in good faith. ContosoTech will perform the services in a
professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.
